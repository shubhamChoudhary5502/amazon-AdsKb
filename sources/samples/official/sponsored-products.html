Sponsored Products

Sponsored Products are cost-per-click (CPC) ads that promote individual product listings on Amazon. You pay only when shoppers click your ad.

Ads appear in shopping results and on product detail pages. Sponsored Products ads are created from your existing product listings, so no custom ad copy or images are required.

Who can use it

Available to professional sellers, vendors, book vendors, and authors. Brand Registry enrollment is not required for Sponsored Products.

Targeting

Choose automatic targeting, where Amazon matches your ads to shopping queries and products, or manual targeting, where you select keywords or products yourself. Campaigns set a daily budget, and you control the maximum cost-per-click bid.
