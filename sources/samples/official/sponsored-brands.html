Sponsored Brands

Sponsored Brands are cost-per-click ads that showcase your brand and a selection of your products. Sponsored Brands require enrollment in Amazon Brand Registry.

Ad formats

Three formats are available: product collection, which promotes multiple products; store spotlight, which drives shoppers to your Store pages; and video, which features a single product with autoplay video in shopping results.

Sponsored Brands ads can link to your Amazon Store or to a custom product landing page. Ads may appear at the top of shopping results, within results, and below results.
