Campaign targeting and bidding

Keyword match types

Manual keyword targeting supports three match types. Broad match reaches shopping queries that contain all the keyword terms in any order, including close variations. Phrase match requires the query to contain the exact phrase or close variations, in order. Exact match requires the query to match the keyword or its close variations exactly.

Product targeting

Product targeting lets you target specific ASINs or entire categories. Category targets can be refined by brand, price range, and star rating.

Negative targeting

Negative keywords, in exact and phrase form, prevent ads from matching unwanted queries. Negative product targeting excludes specific ASINs or brands.

Dynamic bidding

Three bidding strategies are available for Sponsored Products. Dynamic bids down only lowers your bid in real time when a click is less likely to convert. Dynamic bids up and down can raise your bid by up to 100% for placement at the top of the first page of shopping results, and by up to 50% for all other placements, and lowers it when conversion is less likely. Fixed bids use your exact bid without real-time adjustment.

Placement adjustments

You can additionally increase bids by placement, by up to 900%, for top of search and product pages placements.

Campaign structure

Campaigns contain ad groups. Ad groups contain the ads and the keyword or product targets that share a common theme. Daily budgets are set at the campaign level, and portfolios group campaigns for organization and budget caps.
