Sponsored Display

Sponsored Display is a self-service display advertising product. Ads can reach audiences both on Amazon and on third-party destinations off Amazon.

Targeting and audiences

Sponsored Display offers contextual targeting, which targets products or categories, and audience targeting, which includes views remarketing, purchases remarketing, and Amazon audiences built from shopping signals.

Pricing

Sponsored Display supports cost-per-click (CPC) bidding and, for campaigns optimized for reach, cost per thousand viewable impressions (vCPM) bidding.

Sponsored Display requires Brand Registry for sellers.
