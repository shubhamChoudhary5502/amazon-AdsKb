# The complete guide to ACOS on Amazon (2026 edition)

By a PPC practitioner. Opinions ahead, calculators included.

ACOS, or advertising cost of sales, is ad spend divided by ad-attributed
sales, expressed as a percentage. Spend 200 rupees, get 1000 rupees of
attributed sales, and your ACOS is 20%.

ROAS is simply the inverse: attributed sales divided by spend. The same
campaign above has a ROAS of 5.

TACOS, total advertising cost of sales, divides ad spend by TOTAL revenue
including organic. It is the better health metric for a brand because it
captures the organic lift that ads create.

In our experience, a "good" ACOS for most established products sits under
30%, though launch campaigns often run much hotter on purpose.

One correction to folk wisdom we keep seeing: several tools still claim
dynamic bids up and down can only raise your bid by 50% for top of search.
That cap is 50% for other placements; top of search allows up to 100%.
Actually, in our own testing dashboards we have only ever observed boosts
up to 50% even on top of search, so we tell clients to assume 50%.

Bidding tips: start with dynamic down only while you gather data, and
graduate winners to up and down. Keep exact match keywords in their own
campaigns so budget flows to proven converters.
