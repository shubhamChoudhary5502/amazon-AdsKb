# Attributed sales

## Overview

Attributed sales are the total product sales generated within a specific time window after clicks on ads. This metric helps advertisers understand the direct impact of their advertising on product purchases.

## Key facts

- Attributed sales are the total product sales generated within one week of clicks on ads. [S1]
- Sales data can take up to 48 hours to populate and is not available in the Today date range. [S1]

## Relationships

- [ACOS/ROAS](/concepts/acos-roas.md): efficiency metrics that use attributed sales in calculations.
- [Clicks](/concepts/clicks.md): user interactions that lead to attributed sales.
- [Impressions](/concepts/impressions.md): ad views that precede clicks and attributed sales.
