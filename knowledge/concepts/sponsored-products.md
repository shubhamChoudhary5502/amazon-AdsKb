# Sponsored Products

## Overview

Sponsored Products is Amazon's core self-service ad product. It promotes
individual product listings inside Amazon's shopping experience and is
usually the first ad type an advertiser runs.

## Key facts

- Sponsored Products are cost-per-click (CPC) ads that promote individual product listings on Amazon and select premium apps and websites. [S1]
- Sponsored Products use cost-per-click (CPC) pricing model. [S1]
- Sponsored Products promote individual product listings on Amazon. [S1]
- Advertisers only pay when shoppers click their Sponsored Products ad. [S1]
- Pricing is cost-per-click: the advertiser pays only when a shopper clicks the ad. [S1]
- Sponsored Products campaigns have no monthly or upfront fees, advertisers pay only when a shopper clicks an ad. [S1]
- You can create a Sponsored Products campaign in just a few minutes with image or video creative. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads are created from existing product listings. [S1]
- Sponsored Products do not require custom ad copy or images. [S1]
- Ads are generated from the existing product listing, so no custom copy or images are required. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads appear at the top of, alongside, or within shopping results and on product pages. [S1]
- Sponsored Products placements include the top of, alongside, or within shopping results and on product pages on Amazon. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads appear in shopping results and on product detail pages. [S1]
- Ads appear in shopping results and on product detail pages, as well as on Amazon-owned sites and third-party destinations. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads appear beyond Amazon on Amazon-owned and operated sites and third-party destinations. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads appear as image or as long-form interactive video. [S1]
- Ads appear as images or video creatives in shopping results and on product detail pages. [S1]
- Sponsored Products supports browser and app experiences across desktop, mobile, and tablet devices. [S1]
- Ads serve on desktop and mobile browsers as well as Amazon mobile app across devices. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads appear on desktop and mobile browsers as well as the Amazon mobile app. [S1]
- Sponsored Products takes shoppers directly to the product detail page to boost sales of individual products. [S1]
- When customers click a Sponsored Products ad, they are directed to the advertised products detail page. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads show multiple product features using images and video to help customers make informed decisions. [S1]
- Targeting is either automatic, where Amazon matches ads to queries and products, or manual, where the advertiser picks keywords or products. [S1]
- Campaigns set a daily budget, and the advertiser controls the maximum cost-per-click bid. [S1]
- Daily budgets are not paced throughout the day and represent a monthly calendar commitment. [S1]
- Sponsored Products provides tools and reports to help optimize campaign performance and measure success. [S1]
- Sponsored Products reports provide insight on sales and performance of advertised products or targets. [S1]
- Sponsored Products reports show how different ad placements are performing. [S1]
- Available for professional sellers, vendors, book vendors, Kindle Direct Publishing (KDP) authors, and agencies. [S1]
- Products must be in eligible categories and eligible for the Featured Offer to advertise. [S1]
- Adult products, used products, refurbished products, and products in closed categories are not eligible. [S1]
- Sponsored Products ads only appear when advertised items are in stock. [S1]
- New sellers can earn up to $1000 in ad credits when they launch and spend on Sponsored Products within 30 days. [S1]
- Sponsored Products campaigns with video saw 67% higher click-through rate compared to campaigns without video. [S1]
- Sponsored Products campaigns with video saw 9% higher conversion rate compared to campaigns without video. [S1]
- Sponsored Products with video saw an average 9% uplift in click-through rate compared to campaigns without video. [S1]
- Campaigns with video saw average +9% uplift in click-through rate (CTR) compared to campaigns without video. [S1]
- Sponsored Products offers three targeting options: automatic, manual, and negative targeting. [S2]
- Automatic targeting matches ads to keywords and products similar to the advertised product based on shopping queries and product information. [S2]
- Automatic targeting uses four default strategies: close match, loose match, substitutes, and complements. [S2]
- Close match automatic targeting shows ads to shoppers using search terms closely related to the advertised product. [S2]
- Loose match automatic targeting shows ads to shoppers using search terms loosely related to the advertised product. [S2]
- Substitutes automatic targeting shows ads to shoppers browsing detail pages of products similar to the advertised product. [S2]
- Complements automatic targeting shows ads to shoppers viewing detail pages of products that complement the advertised product. [S2]
- Manual targeting allows advertisers to manually select which keywords or products to target. [S2]
- Amazon recommends letting automatic campaigns run for about two weeks before creating manual campaigns. [S2]
