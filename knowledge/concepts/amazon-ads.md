# Amazon Ads

## Overview

Amazon Ads is a comprehensive advertising platform that allows brands, agencies, and third-party sellers to create targeted advertisements. It provides tools for keyword targeting and various ad formats to reach customers at different stages of their shopping journey.

## Key facts

- Amazon PPC advertising allows brands, agencies, and third-party sellers to target specific keywords for advertisements that appear in search results and competitor product listings. [S1]
- Three out of four Amazon sellers of all types and sizes utilize Amazon PPC advertising. [S1]
- Amazon offers AI-powered image generation to create lifestyle imagery that improves ad performance. [S1]
