# Display ads

## Overview

Display ads represent Amazon's enhanced display advertising platform that connects advertisers with audiences across Amazon's properties including Twitch, Fire TV, and Echo Show, as well as premium placements across the open internet.

## Key facts

- The new platform connects display campaigns with customers across Amazon properties including Twitch, Fire TV, and Echo Show, as well as premium placements across the open internet. [S1]
- To create new display campaigns, go to Campaigns, click Create Campaign, and then click Display. [S1]
- Display ads are flexible ad formats that help brands reach relevant audiences wherever they spend time. [S1]
- Display ads support both Amazon-generated creative and custom creative assets. [S1]
