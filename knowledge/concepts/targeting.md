# Targeting

## Overview

Targeting defines the context in which ads appear by matching keywords to customer shopping queries. It is a fundamental concept across Amazon Advertising products.

## Key facts

- Targeting defines the context in which ads appear on Amazon. [S1]
- Sponsored Products offers three targeting options: Automatic, Manual, and Negative targeting. [S1]
- Automatic targeting uses four default strategies: Close match, Loose match, Substitutes, and Complements. [S1]
- Close match automatic targeting shows ads to shoppers using search terms closely related to the advertised product. [S1]
- Loose match automatic targeting shows ads to shoppers using search terms loosely related to the advertised product. [S1]
- Substitutes automatic targeting shows ads on detail pages of products similar to the advertised product. [S1]
- Complements automatic targeting shows ads on detail pages of products that complement the advertised product. [S1]
- Amazon recommends letting automatic targeting campaigns run for about two weeks before creating manual campaigns. [S1]
- Manual targeting allows advertisers to manually select which keywords or products to target. [S1]
- Branded keywords are directly associated with a brand or a brand being sold. [S1]
- Bidding on branded keywords can be used as a brand defense strategy since competitors may bid on your branded keywords. [S1]
- Category keywords are non-branded keywords that relate to any product or category. [S1]
- Short-tail category keywords have 1-3 words while long-tail keywords have three or more words. [S1]
- Six campaign strategies include: Expand, Promote, Protect, Conquest, Upsell, and Cross-sell. [S1]
- Amazon recommends starting small with one targeting type and testing different targeting types. [S1]
- Sponsored Products offers flexible keyword and product targeting options and recommendations. [S3]
- You select products to advertise and choose keywords to target, or let Amazon systems target relevant keywords automatically. [S3]
- Sponsored Brands reaches shoppers who matter most through precise keyword and product targeting. [S2]
- Customer shopping queries are words and phrases Amazon customers use to look for products. [S1]
- Sponsored Products manual targeting allows selecting keywords or products. [S3]

## Community claims

- Automatic targeting involves Amazon's algorithm determining keywords related to product listings and adjusting ads based on shopper data. [S4]
- Substitutes targeting reaches shoppers considering products similar to yours but sold by different brands. [S4]
- Complements targeting targets shoppers viewing detail pages of products that complement yours. [S4]
- Manual targeting involves hand-selecting keywords to bid on and only appears if search terms match chosen keywords. [S4]
- Keyword research for manual targeting can be performed using related keyword searches or reverse ASIN lookups. [S4]
- Reverse ASIN lookup reveals keywords for which a competitor's product is ranking. [S4]
