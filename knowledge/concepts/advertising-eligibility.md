# Advertising eligibility

## Overview

Who can run which ad product, and where Brand Registry sits as the gate.
This is the first question every new Indian SMB seller asks, and the
answer differs per product.

## Key facts

- Sponsored Products is available to professional sellers, vendors, book vendors, and authors, with no Brand Registry requirement. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands requires enrollment in Amazon Brand Registry. [S2]
- Sponsored Display requires Brand Registry for sellers. [S3]

## Relationships

- [Sponsored Products](/concepts/sponsored-products.md): the no-Brand-Registry entry point.
- [Sponsored Brands](/concepts/sponsored-brands.md): gated by Brand Registry.
- [Sponsored Display](/concepts/sponsored-display.md): gated by Brand Registry for sellers.
