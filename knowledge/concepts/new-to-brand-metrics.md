# New-to-brand metrics

## Overview

New-to-brand metrics measure orders and sales of products generated from first-time customers of the brand on Amazon. They supplement traditional ACOS/ROAS by measuring brand growth impact and customer acquisition.

## Key facts

- New-to-brand metrics measure orders and sales of products generated from first-time customers of the brand on Amazon. [S1]
- New-to-brand metrics track number of first-time orders for products within the brand within a 12-month look-back window. [S1]
- New-to-brand metrics track percentage of total orders that are new-to-brand orders within 12 months. [S1]
- New-to-brand metrics track total sales of new-to-brand orders within 12 months. [S1]
- New-to-brand metrics track percentage of total sales that are new-to-brand sales within 12 months. [S1]
- New-to-brand metrics use a 12-month look-back window for measuring first-time customer purchases. [S1]
- New-to-brand metrics supplement ACOS or ROAS to measure brand growth impact. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands helps drive brand discovery with shoppers in the discovery stage and helps brands reach new customers. [S1]
- Driving first-time purchases can cost more than driving repeat purchases, so ACOS or ROAS alone would not measure brand audience growth impact. [S1]
