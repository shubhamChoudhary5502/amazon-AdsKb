# Impressions

## Overview

Impressions represent the number of times ads were displayed to potential customers. This metric is fundamental for understanding ad visibility and reach, though it includes validation processes to ensure accuracy.

## Key facts

- Impressions represent the number of times ads were displayed and can take up to three days to remove invalid clicks. [S1]

## Relationships

- [Clicks](/concepts/clicks.md): user interactions that occur after impressions.
- [Attributed sales](/concepts/attributed-sales.md): purchases that result from the impression-click-conversion path.
- [ACOS/ROAS](/concepts/acos-roas.md): efficiency metrics that factor in impression-based costs.
