# Clicks

## Overview

Clicks represent user interactions with advertisements where a shopper clicks on an ad to visit the advertised product. This metric may be adjusted during a validation period to remove invalid or fraudulent clicks.

## Key facts

- Clicks from the last three days may be adjusted due to click invalidation. [S1]

## Relationships

- [Impressions](/concepts/impressions.md): ad views that precede clicks.
- [Attributed sales](/concepts/attributed-sales.md): purchases that result from clicks.
- [ACOS/ROAS](/concepts/acos-roas.md): efficiency metrics that use click costs in calculations.
