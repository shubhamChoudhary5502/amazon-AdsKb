# Sponsored Brands video

## Overview

Sponsored Brands video is a powerful storytelling tool that uses rich autoplay video content to help drive brand recognition for new brands and enhance reach for existing brands. It enables featuring multiple products and linking to Brand Stores.

## Key facts

- Sponsored Brands provides Video Generator in Creative tools tab to assist with building video creative. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands offers video creative production and editing services through Amazon. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands provides video as added value with qualifying campaigns or as affordable at-cost fee-based services. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video uses rich autoplay video content to help drive brand recognition for new brands and enhance reach for existing brands. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video is a powerful storytelling tool to initiate, cultivate, and maintain relationships with shoppers. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video enables featuring multiple products and linking to Brand Stores. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video provides exclusive access to high-visibility placements. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video enables featuring up to three products within ads when linking to Brand Stores. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video ads could appear above, in the middle, or at the bottom of Amazon search results. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video enables promoting eye-catching video with flexibility of showcasing up to three products. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands video enables driving traffic to Brand Store to explore full range of product offerings in dedicated brand environment. [S1]
- Sponsored Brands provides guide for getting started with Sponsored Brands video ads. [S1]
